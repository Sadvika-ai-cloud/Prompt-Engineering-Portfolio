## Prompt-01 : Study Planner

Act as an experienced GATE CSE topper and study mentor.

Create a 1-year GATE preparation plan starting from today. I can study 4 hours every day, and I need to complete all 12 GATE subjects along with revision, PYQ practice, and mock tests.

Design the plan in the following structure:

1. Month-wise subject distribution
2. Weekly study targets
3. Daily 4-hour study schedule
4. Dedicated PYQ practice
5. Revision schedule after completing each subject
6. Mock-test schedule with increasing frequency
7. Monthly progress checkpoints
8. Buffer time for backlogs and difficult topics

Prioritize high-weightage and interconnected subjects appropriately. Make sure the workload is realistic within 4 hours per day and that the final months focus more heavily on revision, PYQs, and full-length mock tests.

Present the final plan in clear tables and include measurable targets for each month.
